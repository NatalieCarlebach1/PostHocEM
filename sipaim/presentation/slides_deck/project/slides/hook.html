PEM · SIPAIM 2026

(About 50 seconds.) Semi-supervised methods such as BCP use a large unlabeled set during training, and then stop using it once training converges. We looked at what a converged model still doesn’t know about that unlabeled data. On the left is an MRI slice; on the right, the per-voxel entropy of the trained BCP model. On three BCP checkpoints, pancreas CT at 20% labels and left atrium at 5% and 10%, 89 to 95 percent of the prediction entropy sits in only 5 to 9 percent of the voxels, and those voxels form a thin shell on the predicted surface, where segmentation errors live. So our question: can a few epochs on that leftover uncertainty improve the model, with no labels at all?
