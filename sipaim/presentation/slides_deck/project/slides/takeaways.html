PEM · SIPAIM 2026

(About 40 seconds.) Three things to take home. First, converged BCP checkpoints leave residual entropy that is spatially structured, in a thin shell on the organ surface, and rho-f measures that concentration without labels. Second, Post-hoc Entropy Minimization uses it cheaply: under a minute of label-free fine-tuning cuts the boundary error by 25 to 44 percent, with the clearest and statistically strongest gain over other post-hoc methods on pancreas. Third, use it with care: check rho-f first and keep the tight mask, because a model with diffuse entropy collapses and looser masks can cost 30 Dice points. The main limitations: our tests are exploratory, the threshold rests on four checkpoints, and the test sets have 18 to 20 volumes. Thank you, and I’m happy to take questions.
