(About 50 seconds.) What does the mask buy? This is the ablation, read at each dataset’s fixed endpoint, not at a peak. Loosening the mask from 0.90 toward 0.999 readmits the saturated interior and approaches the unmasked loss, and the number of degraded cells rises along that path, from zero to three. The failures are large: the loosest mask costs 30 Dice points on LA 10 percent. Tau-m = 0.90 is the only threshold that never degrades a BCP checkpoint, and it also has the highest mean gain. 0.95 has the single largest cell, but it beats 0.99 in only three of six, so we don’t recommend it. On the robust instance-normalized pancreas checkpoint the unmasked loss is slightly better. So the mask is insurance against collapse on brittle checkpoints, not a source of gain.
