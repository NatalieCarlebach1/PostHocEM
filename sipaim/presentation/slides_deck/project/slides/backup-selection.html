PEM · SIPAIM 2026

Backup for questions on selection. The learning rate and mask threshold are chosen on the labeled training volumes, by their Dice change; those volumes never enter the PEM loss but did train the base checkpoint, so they are not an independent validation set. Five-fold cross-validation was impossible with four volumes on LA 5 percent. As a retrospective check, the labeled-volume change ranks the 33 configurations the way the test change does: Spearman 0.78, 0.75 and 0.99, and 0.67 to 0.96 with outright collapses removed, so it is not just collapse detection. The budgets came from the submitted protocol, and the pancreas BCP is our reproduction saved at its best test Dice.
