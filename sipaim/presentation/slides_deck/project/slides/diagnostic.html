PEM · SIPAIM 2026

(About 60 seconds.) Because sharpening hurts when the uncertainty is not boundary-shaped, we want a check we can run before fine-tuning, without labels. That is rho-f: the fraction of the total prediction entropy held by the low-confidence voxels, from one forward pass over the unlabeled data. The three BCP checkpoints all score about 0.9 or higher. A supervised-only network trained on the same 12 pancreas labels scores 0.55: its uncertainty is scattered through the interior. And on that checkpoint PEM fails badly: the very setting that improves BCP pancreas by about one Dice point collapses the supervised model from 75.7 to 17.3 Dice. Two caveats: the 0.85 threshold rests on these four checkpoints only, and rho-f has no spatial term; that the uncertain voxels sit on the boundary we show separately, with their distance to it.
