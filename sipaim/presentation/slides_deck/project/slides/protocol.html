PEM · SIPAIM 2026

(About 40 seconds.) A word on how the settings were chosen, since it matters for every number that follows. The learning rate and the mask threshold are chosen on the labeled training volumes, by the change in their Dice; those volumes never enter the PEM loss, which sees only unlabeled data. The two baselines, pseudo-label fine-tuning and self-consistency, go through exactly the same rule and grid, and all methods report the final epoch, averaged over three seeds. In full transparency, this is a corrected reanalysis: the epoch budgets and grid bounds came from earlier sweeps that saw test results, and our pancreas BCP was saved at its best test Dice. So we treat the significance tests as exploratory.
