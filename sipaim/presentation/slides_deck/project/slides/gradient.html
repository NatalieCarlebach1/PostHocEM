(About 60 seconds.) Why does this work without any stabilizer? Look at the gradient of a voxel’s entropy with respect to its logit: minus z times p times one minus p. It vanishes on the saturated interior, where p is near 0 or 1, and at maximum ambiguity, p = 0.5. It peaks at p of about 0.82. So the gradient is already concentrated on a band of partly decided voxels next to the decision surface, the shell from the previous slide, with no mask at all. What does the mask add? At 0.90 the gradient is still 88 percent of its peak, so the mask both removes those voxels and renormalizes the loss over the ones it keeps. Our ablation does not separate the two effects, so we treat the mask as insurance against collapse; you will see the evidence in a moment.
