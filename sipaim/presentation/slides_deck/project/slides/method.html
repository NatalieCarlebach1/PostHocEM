PEM · SIPAIM 2026

(About 60 seconds.) The method, Post-hoc Entropy Minimization or PEM, is deliberately simple. Take any converged SSL checkpoint; in our experiments that is BCP, the released checkpoints for left atrium and our reproduction of BCP for pancreas. Fine-tune it on the unlabeled volumes with a single loss: the mean entropy of the voxels whose confidence is at most tau-m, which we set to 0.90. There are no labels, no pseudo-labels, no teacher and no change to the network. Stop after a fixed budget, two epochs for pancreas and five for left atrium, and report the final epoch. The fine-tuning itself takes under a minute on one GPU. Entropy minimization is classical; what is new is the regime: applying it after convergence, when the remaining entropy already sits on the boundary.
